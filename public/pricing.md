# Fee Schedule & Brokerage Terms — VALOREA MARKET

> Governed by Swiss law and ICC 400/500/600 Irrevocable Master Fee Protection Agreement (IMFPA) standards.

## 1. Catalog & Marketplace Access
- **Price**: $0 (Free)
- **Included**: Public viewing of verified commodity allocations, specifications, origin certificates, and delivery ports.
- **Requirements**: No payment required for browsing. Corporate registration required for ICPO submission.

## 2. Institutional Brokerage & Matching Fees
VALOREA MARKET operates on standard international physical commodity brokerage fee benchmarks:
- **Liquid Hydrocarbons (Crude Oil, EN590 Diesel, Jet A1)**:
  - Benchmark Fee: $1.00 to $2.00 per Metric Ton (or $0.10 to $0.30 per Barrel), split between buyer-side and seller-side intermediary chains as stipulated in the agreed IMFPA.
- **Fertilizers & Nutrients (Granular Urea 46%, DAP)**:
  - Benchmark Fee: 0.5% to 1.5% of CIF contract value.
- **Non-Ferrous Metals (Copper Cathodes, Aluminium)**:
  - Benchmark Fee: 0.5% to 1.0% of LME cash-settled benchmark value.

## 3. Deal Room & Compliance Verification
- **KYC / KYB Sanctions Screening**: Covered within transaction clearing.
- **E2EE Encrypted Deal Rooms**: Included for active contracting parties with verified Proof of Funds (POF) and Proof of Product (POP).
- **Settlement Method**: Brokerage fees are automatically disbursed by the designated paying bank simultaneously with the primary MT103 wire settlement upon presentation of clean shipping documents.
